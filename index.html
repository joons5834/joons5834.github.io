rclone for gdrive

rclone for gdrive is a personal backup tool operated by a single individual on a private home server. It copies files from the operator's own Google Drive account to the operator's own Koofr account for backup purposes.

What it does

With the operator's explicit consent, it reads files in the operator's Google Drive and copies them to the operator's Koofr storage. It runs on a schedule and is not distributed to or used by the public.

Data

No data is collected, stored, or shared with any third party.
