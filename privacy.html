Privacy Policy

This policy applies to rclone for gdrive, a personal backup tool operated by a single individual for their own use.

What data is accessed

With the user's explicit consent, this application reads files stored in the user's Google Drive account and copies them to the user's own Koofr account.

How data is processed and stored

No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any third-party server. Files are copied directly between the user's own cloud storage accounts, from the user's own home server.

Data sharing

No data is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party.

Retention and deletion

No copies of the user's data are retained beyond the user's own Koofr account. Access may be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
